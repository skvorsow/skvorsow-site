About

David Skvorsow

David Skvorsow works with sculpture, painting and digital art. Born in Kazakhstan in 1996, he lives and works in Saint Petersburg.

He graduated from the Department of Monumental and Decorative Painting at the Stieglitz Academy in 2023 and studied at Weißensee Kunsthochschule Berlin through a student exchange programme.

He has exhibited in Russia and Europe. His works are held in private collections in Russia, Italy and Germany.
