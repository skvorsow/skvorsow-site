ASTROFLEUR

Sculpture / Digital

ASTROFLEUR grew out of studies of radial symmetry, the ornamental structure of the mandala and the possibility of turning a sacred image into a physical object.

Made from chrome-plated steel, it sits between an industrial surface, a decorative motif and an object of worship: precise, sharp and without an obvious function. The work can stand on a plinth, hang from chains or be mounted on a wall.
