The System
Learns You

Drawing / Digital

The System Learns You is a series of drawings placed on physical and digital objects. An image can live on paper, painted fabric, a wall, an installation or a simulated material surface.

The series follows the moment a drawing stops being a flat image and starts behaving like a thing — with a surface, a scale, a sense of weight and the possibility of physical presence.
