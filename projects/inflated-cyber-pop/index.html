Inflated
Cyber Pop

Digital sculpture

Inflated Cyber Pop works between sculpture, painting and the object. These forms might be painted soft sculptures, canvases inflated from within, fragments of clothing, scenery or characters.

They never quite settle into any of these categories. The project experiments with soft volume, surface, ornament, the body and degrees of abstraction — almost an object, almost a painting, almost a body.
